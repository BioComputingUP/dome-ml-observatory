Governance

DOME Observatory is hosted and run by the UNIPD Biocomputing Lab (see the team for who does what day to day). Independent oversight of the project's direction sits with the Scientific Advisory Board below.

Scientific Advisory Board

The Board advises the DOME services as a whole — the DOME Recommendations, the DOME Registry and DOME Observatory — rather than any one of them individually. It provides independent strategic advice and critical evaluation from internationally recognized experts spanning industry, academia and scholarly publishing, reviewing direction and priorities and weighing in on major decisions before they are set. Day-to-day development, curation and infrastructure decisions remain with the teams running each service.
