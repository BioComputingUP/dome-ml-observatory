Licensing & citation

Licence

The curated metadata served by DOME Observatory — screened, classified and enriched record data — is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0) . You are free to share and adapt the material for any purpose, even commercially, as long as you give appropriate credit.

This is not the same as each paper's own licence

CC BY 4.0 covers Observatory's metadata — the classification, enrichment and curation this project adds. It does not relicense the underlying papers themselves, which each carry their own terms. Every record's actual access licence (where recorded) is shown on its record page — some papers are open access under CC BY or similar terms, others remain under the publisher's standard copyright. Check the specific record before reusing the paper's own text or figures.

Data source & attribution

The publication metadata underlying this corpus is sourced largely from Europe PMC, and the screening and enrichment described on the About page are performed against that source data. Europe PMC should be cited alongside DOME Observatory in any work that reuses this corpus. 

Rosonovski S, Levchenko M, Bhatnagar R, Chandrasekaran U, Faulk L, Hassan I,
Jeffryes M, Mubashar SI, Nassar M, Palanisamy MJ, Parkin M, Poluru J, Rogers F,
Saha S, Selim M, Shafique Z, Ide-Smith M, Stephenson D, Tirunagari S,
Venkatesan A, Xing L, Harrison M. "Europe PMC in 2023."
Nucleic Acids Research 52(D1):D1668-D1676 (2024). doi:10.1093/nar/gkad1085

Europe PMC content carries its own terms — see their copyright notice. Observatory's CC BY 4.0 licence covers the curation layer this project adds, not the upstream records.

Citing a specific record

Use the BibTeX or RIS export on any record page to cite an individual paper — that citation is for the paper itself, under its own terms, not for Observatory's metadata about it.
