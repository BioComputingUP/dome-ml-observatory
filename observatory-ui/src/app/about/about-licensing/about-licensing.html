Licensing & citation

What the licence covers

DOME Observatory is a discovery layer over publication metadata, not a repository of publications. The layer this project adds is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0) :

  • the AI/ML screening decisions and their rationales,
  • the enrichment tags — domain, learning paradigm, model family and model type,
  • the controlled vocabularies and record schema,
  • and the aggregate statistics, journal rankings and facet counts.

You are free to share and adapt that material for any purpose, including commercially, as long as you give appropriate credit.

This covers the data and documentation. Creative Commons licences are not intended for software, so CC BY 4.0 is not asserted over the application source code; no separate code licence has been declared yet.

Where the underlying data comes from

Each record also carries bibliographic metadata and an abstract that this project did not create and does not own — CC BY 4.0 does not extend to it. Roughly 93.5% of the abstracts in the corpus are retrieved from Europe PMC through their public APIs, with the remainder from Crossref and PubMed, and a small share of records carrying no abstract at all. Each record's open-access flag and licence string also come from the Europe PMC licensing lookup.

Europe PMC should be cited alongside DOME Observatory in any work that reuses this corpus.

Rosonovski S, Levchenko M, Bhatnagar R, Chandrasekaran U, Faulk L, Hassan I,
Jeffryes M, Mubashar SI, Nassar M, Palanisamy MJ, Parkin M, Poluru J, Rogers F,
Saha S, Selim M, Shafique Z, Ide-Smith M, Stephenson D, Tirunagari S,
Venkatesan A, Xing L, Harrison M. "Europe PMC in 2023."
Nucleic Acids Research 52(D1):D1668-D1676 (2024). doi:10.1093/nar/gkad1085

Europe PMC's own terms govern that upstream content — see their copyright notice. Two points from it shape how this corpus may be reused: Europe PMC places no blanket open licence on the metadata and abstracts it serves, so standard copyright should be assumed for an article unless that article says otherwise; and automated retrieval must go through their APIs rather than by crawling their site, which is how this corpus was assembled. Where a record came from Crossref or PubMed instead, those sources' terms apply in the same way.

Full text is linked, never hosted

Observatory stores title, abstract, authors, year, journal and citation count. It does not hold, mirror or redistribute the full text of any publication. Every route to the article itself is an outbound link to the source that holds it — Europe PMC, PubMed, PMC, or the publisher via DOI — and access to that full text is governed entirely by whoever hosts it.

Each paper's own licence still governs that paper

Access terms vary across the corpus. Around two thirds of the source corpus is flagged open access, and each record's licence string — cc by, for example — is shown on its record page where a licence lookup matched. Some papers are open access under CC BY or similar terms; others remain under a publisher's standard copyright; for some, no licence information was available at all, and an empty field means unknown, not unrestricted.

Check a specific paper's own terms at the source before reusing its abstract, text or figures. A CC BY 4.0 licence on Observatory's classification of a paper says nothing about what you may do with the paper.

Citing a specific record

Use the BibTeX or RIS export on any record page to cite an individual paper — that citation is for the paper itself, under its own terms, not for Observatory's metadata about it.

Corrections

If you believe a record misrepresents your work, or that anything here is redistributed in a way its licence does not permit, please open an issue or email contact@dome-ml.org, and we will correct or remove it.
