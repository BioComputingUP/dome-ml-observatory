What is collected today

DOME Observatory has no user accounts and requires no sign-in. The personal data we process about you as a visitor is standard web server access logs — IP address, timestamp, requested URL and browser user-agent string — generated automatically by the web server for every request, as almost every website does.

Lawful basis

Server log processing is necessary for our legitimate interest in operating, securing and troubleshooting the service. Matomo relies on the same basis — our legitimate interest in understanding aggregate usage. Because it is cookieless and stores nothing on your device, it needs no consent under the ePrivacy Directive, which is precisely why it is the only analytics we use.

International data transfers

No data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, with no exceptions and no onward transfer to any third country. Fonts and icons are served from this origin rather than from an external CDN, and analytics run on a University of Padua server rather than a third-party service, so using this site sends your data to no other company and to no other jurisdiction.

Who has access

Server logs are accessible only to authorised technical staff operating the service.

Retention

Server access logs are retained only as long as needed for security and operational troubleshooting, and are not kept indefinitely. Analytics data is kept in aggregate for trend reporting; because it is cookieless and IP-anonymised, it cannot be traced back to an individual visitor.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to data portability and objection, subject to applicable exemptions. Contact the Data Controller above to exercise these rights, or the DPO if you have concerns about how your request was handled. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).
