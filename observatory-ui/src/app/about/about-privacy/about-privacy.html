What is collected today

DOME Observatory has no user accounts and requires no sign-in. The personal data we process about you as a visitor is standard web server access logs — IP address, timestamp, requested URL and browser user-agent string — generated automatically by the web server for every request, as almost every website does. Nothing else about your visit is recorded, and no third-party service is contacted when you load a page: fonts, icons and all other assets are served from this site.

Separately, the corpus itself contains personal data about the authors of published papers — names, and occasionally a corresponding-author email address that appears within a published abstract. This information is reproduced as published by the source journal and obtained via Europe PMC; it is processed for scientific research purposes. See Licensing for how the metadata may be reused, and contact us at contact@dome-ml.org to request correction or removal of a record.

Planned: usage analytics Not yet active

To understand how the site is used, we intend to add:

  • Matomo — a self-hosted analytics platform, configured to anonymise IP addresses and avoid identifying individual visitors where possible.
  • Google Analytics — a third-party analytics service. Because it sets non-essential cookies and transfers data outside the EU/EEA, it will only load after you actively consent via a cookie banner — never by default.

Neither is active yet. This page will be updated, and a consent banner put in place, before either goes live — not after.

Lawful basis

Server log processing is necessary for our legitimate interest in operating, securing and troubleshooting the service. Once active, Google Analytics will rely on your consent, given via the cookie banner; Matomo, configured cookieless as intended, will rely on legitimate interest in understanding aggregate usage.

International data transfers

No data is transferred outside the EU/EEA. The site loads no third-party resources — fonts and icons are served from this origin rather than from an external CDN, so loading a page contacts no other company. Once Google Analytics is active, data it collects will be transferred to Google LLC in the United States, under Google's standard contractual safeguards. This section will be expanded with the specific safeguards in place once that integration ships.

Who has access

Server logs are accessible only to authorised technical staff operating the service.

Retention

Server access logs are retained only as long as needed for security and operational troubleshooting, and are not kept indefinitely.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to data portability and objection, subject to applicable exemptions. Contact the Data Controller above to exercise these rights, or the DPO if you have concerns about how your request was handled. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).
