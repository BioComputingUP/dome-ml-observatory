How did DOME come to be?

DOME guidelines were initially formulated through the ELIXIR ML focus group after the publication of a comment calling for the establishment of standards for ML in biology [PMID: 31548714]. Over 700 national experts participate in the development and operation of national services that contribute to data access, integration, training and analysis for the research community. Over 50 of these experts involved in the field of ML have established an ELIXIR ML focus group which held a number of meetings to develop and refine the recommendations based on a broad consensus among them.
