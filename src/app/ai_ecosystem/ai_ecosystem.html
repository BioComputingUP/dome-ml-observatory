OSAI Recommendations

The Open and Sustainable AI (OSAI) framework proposes the following recommendations to guide the development and deployment of AI systems in a manner that is both ethically sound and environmentally conscious:

R1: Foster Open Collaboration and Knowledge Sharing.

Encourage the use of open licenses, platforms, and standards to facilitate widespread access and contribution to AI research and development. Promote transparency in methodologies and data sources.

R2: Prioritize Ethical AI Development and Deployment.

Integrate ethical considerations, including fairness, accountability, and transparency (FAT), throughout the AI lifecycle. Develop mechanisms for redress and ensure human oversight.

R3: Ensure Data Privacy and Security.

Implement robust data governance frameworks that protect individual privacy and ensure data security. Employ techniques like federated learning and differential privacy where appropriate.

R4: Promote AI Literacy and Public Engagement.

Invest in education and public awareness programs to demystify AI and empower citizens to participate in discussions about its societal impact. Foster dialogue between developers, policymakers, and the public.

R5: Address Environmental Sustainability.

Develop and promote energy-efficient AI models and hardware. Assess and mitigate the environmental footprint of AI systems, from data centers to device deployment.

R6: Support Sustainable Funding and Infrastructure.

Encourage long-term investment in open AI research and infrastructure. Explore sustainable funding models that are not solely reliant on large corporations or short-term grants.

R7: Champion Inclusivity and Diversity.

Actively work to reduce bias in AI systems and datasets. Promote diversity within AI development teams and ensure that AI technologies benefit all segments of society.

R8: Establish Clear Governance and Regulatory Frameworks.

Develop adaptive and globally-coordinated governance structures that can keep pace with AI advancements. Ensure that regulations foster innovation while safeguarding against risks.

R9: Encourage Interdisciplinary Research and Application.

Foster collaboration between AI researchers and experts from other domains (e.g., social sciences, humanities, environmental science) to address complex challenges and ensure responsible AI application.
