A Disclosure Framework Built on Four Pillars

Data

Ensuring high-quality data, proper preprocessing, and transparent handling to build a foundation of trust for your research discoveries.

Optimization

Guiding the selection of appropriate optimization strategies to avoid common pitfalls and ensure your model learns effectively.

Model

Promoting clarity in model architecture, interpretability where required, and responsible dissemination of trained models.

Evaluation

Implementing valid assessment methodologies with comprehensive metrics to prove the true performance and generalizability of your final model.
