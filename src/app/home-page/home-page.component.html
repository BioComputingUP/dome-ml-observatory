Recommendations Focusing on Three Core Areas

Improving
Reusability

By adopting open standards and providing comprehensive documentation, we make AI models and datasets easier for others to find, adapt, and build upon, accelerating the pace of innovation.

Increasing Reproducibility

Provide a clear, step-by-step record of your methodology—from data preprocessing to model evaluation—allowing others to verify your findings and build a more robust scientific foundation.

Accelerating Environmental Sustainability

Encourage the reporting of computational costs and the use of more efficient architectures to reduce the carbon footprint of AI development and deployment.

Enhance Your Research & Build Community Trust

Be Open & Transparent

Champion clear documentation and open access to data, models, and code to foster collaboration, verification, and trust within the scientific community.

Be Fair & Inclusive

Actively work to mitigate harmful biases and ensure that AI systems are designed to be accessible, equitable, and beneficial to all members of society.

Be Accountable & Robust

Establish clear governance structures and implement rigorous evaluation methodologies to ensure models are reliable, secure, and perform as intended.

Be Sustainable & Efficient

Promote the development and use of computationally efficient methods and be mindful of resource usage to minimize the environmental and economic costs of AI.
