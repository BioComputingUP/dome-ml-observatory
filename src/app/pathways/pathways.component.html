Implementation Pathways

Practical steps to implement OSAI recommendations

These implementation pathways provide concrete steps and resources to help you apply the OSAI recommendations in your AI/ML research workflows and systems.

Reusability Pathway

Building AI systems that others can easily find, understand, and adapt for their own research is essential for advancing scientific progress. Follow these steps to ensure your AI models and datasets are reusable.

1Standardize Your Metadata

Create and share standardized metadata for your models and datasets using recommended frameworks such as AI model cards, dataset datasheets, or domain-specific standards.

Key Actions:
  • Document your model's intended use, limitations, and performance metrics
  • Describe your dataset's collection methodology, biases, and usage restrictions
  • Use established templates from your community or domain
Use DOME Registry for Metadata Documentation

2Register Your Research Assets

Make your models, datasets, and code discoverable by registering them in community repositories and catalogues with appropriate metadata.

Key Actions:
  • Submit your AI model to model repositories like Hugging Face or BioModels
  • Share your datasets in domain-relevant repositories with rich metadata
  • Register your software in directories like bio.tools or GitHub

3Document Your Training Process

Provide comprehensive documentation of your model training process, including preprocessing steps, architecture decisions, hyperparameters, and validation approaches.

Key Actions:
  • Create clear documentation using Jupyter notebooks or similar tools
  • Include detailed explanations of design choices and their rationale
  • Document data preprocessing steps and their impact on model performance

Reproducibility Pathway

Enabling others to verify and build upon your research findings is crucial for scientific integrity. This pathway helps ensure your AI research can be reliably reproduced.

1Disclose All Methods and Parameters

Document your complete workflow with sufficient detail for others to reproduce your results, including detailed methods, configuration parameters, and validation procedures.

Key Actions:
  • Record hyperparameters, initialization strategies, and optimization techniques
  • Document data splits, preprocessing, and augmentation methods
  • Describe your evaluation metrics and validation process

2Ensure Code and Environment Portability

Package your code, dependencies, and runtime environment to ensure your research can be run consistently across different computing systems.

Key Actions:
  • Use containerization tools like Docker or Singularity
  • Create environment files (requirements.txt, environment.yml) with specific versions
  • Document system requirements and hardware specifications

3Use Standardized Benchmarks

Validate your models using standardized, community-accepted benchmarks and evaluation protocols to facilitate accurate comparisons with other methods.

Key Actions:
  • Select appropriate benchmark datasets for your domain
  • Follow established evaluation protocols
  • Report performance across multiple metrics and trials

Environmental Sustainability Pathway

Developing AI systems with environmental impact in mind helps reduce the growing carbon footprint of machine learning. Follow these steps to make your AI research more environmentally sustainable.

1Implement Green AI Development Techniques

Adopt development approaches that reduce computational requirements while maintaining performance.

Key Actions:
  • Use transfer learning instead of training from scratch
  • Apply model pruning and knowledge distillation
  • Optimize your model architecture for efficiency
  • Consider Frugal AI practices that emphasize efficiency

2Choose and Optimize Hardware

Select appropriate hardware and optimize its usage to reduce energy consumption and carbon emissions.

Key Actions:
  • Select energy-efficient hardware appropriate for your tasks
  • Use specialized accelerators (e.g., GPUs, TPUs) efficiently
  • Consider cloud providers with renewable energy commitments
  • Schedule training during off-peak hours or in regions with cleaner energy

3Measure and Report Environmental Impact

Track and transparently report the environmental impact of your AI development process.

Key Actions:
  • Use tools like Carbontracker or Code Carbon to measure emissions
  • Report energy consumption, carbon emissions, and compute hours
  • Include environmental impact statements in your publications
  • Share your impact reduction strategies with the community
